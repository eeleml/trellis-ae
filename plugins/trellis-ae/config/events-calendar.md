# Trellis Important Events Calendar (Amazon seasonality)

Used by `ob-external-research` (detect a brand's relevant events) and `ob-messaging` (anchor the opener +
drive urgency). US-market defaults; adjust to the brand's actual selling calendar if research shows it.

## Timing rule
Anchor a contact to a seasonal event ONLY while its **outreach window** is open. The window opens
**~3 months before** the event peak and **closes ~1 month before** it — after that there isn't enough
runway to make meaningful pricing/ads changes in time. Once a window closes, **roll to the next open
event**. Off-window, don't force a seasonal angle. Drive urgency with the closing window ("now's the
window to move the numbers; by [month] it's too late to make real changes in time").

## Which audit to offer
Pick the single most relevant one: a **pricing audit** for pricing / Buy-Box-led pain, an **ads audit**
for ad-efficiency or scaling-into-demand pain. (For a demand ramp into an event, ads is usually the lever.)

## Calendar
| Event | Peak | Outreach window (open → close) | Brand types it applies to | Angle |
|-------|------|--------------------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| Prime Day | mid-Jul | mid-Apr → mid-Jun | nearly all sellers | scale into the spike without ACoS blowout; don't over-discount |
| Back-to-school | early Sep | early Jun → end Jul | school supplies, kids apparel, electronics, dorm/home, snacks, backpacks | dial in pricing/ads while there's runway |
| Oct Big Deal Days (Prime Big Deal Days) | mid-Oct | mid-Jul → mid-Sep | nearly all | second Prime event; margin protection |
| BFCM (Black Friday / Cyber Monday) | late Nov | late Aug → late Oct | nearly all; gifting especially | peak ad costs + Buy Box wars |
| Holiday / Christmas | mid-Dec | mid-Sep → mid-Nov | gifting, toys, electronics, beauty sets, home | demand + competition surge |
| New Year "new you" | early Jan | early Oct → early Dec | fitness, supplements, wellness, organization/home | resolution demand |
| Valentine's Day | mid-Feb | mid-Nov → mid-Jan | gifting, jewelry, chocolate, beauty, flowers | short sharp window |
| Mother's / Father's Day | May / Jun | Feb → Apr | gifting, beauty, grooming, tools | gifting window |
| Summer (outdoor / sun) | Jun–Jul | Mar → May | outdoor, sports, sun/UPF, grills, travel, beauty | seasonal peak ramp |
| Amazon Spring Sale | late Mar | late Dec → late Feb | broad | spring deal event |

Notes:
- A brand can have more than one relevant event; use whichever window is open now.
- Match the angle to the chosen value prop and the audit offered (pricing vs ads).
